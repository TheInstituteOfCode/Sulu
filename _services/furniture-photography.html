- q: What is the difference between a furniture lookbook, lifestyle and editorial photoshoot? a: >-

A furniture lookbook usually focuses on presenting a collection as a cohesive visual story, while lifestyle photography shows the furniture within a more lived-in environment and editorial photography gives the creative direction more room to explore.

The right approach depends on how the images will be used. A furniture brand launching a new collection might need clean product images for ecommerce, a lookbook to present the collection as a whole and more editorial campaign imagery for marketing. At Suluh, we can combine these approaches within one production when it makes sense.

- q: Can you shoot commercial furniture photography at a luxury villa or Airbnb in Bali? a: >-

Booking a beautiful villa on Airbnb does not necessarily mean it is suitable for commercial furniture photography. Many properties have restrictions around commercial shoots, external furniture being brought onto the property or existing furniture being moved. There is also a practical side to consider: some villas that look incredible online can be surprisingly small, difficult to access with large furniture, or have challenging natural light once you arrive.

For that reason, it is worth checking the property in person before committing to a shoot and confirming that commercial photography, external furniture and any required set changes are permitted.

Suluh can produce commercial furniture photography at suitable luxury villas and private properties across Bali, provided the location allows commercial photography and the necessary permissions are in place. We work with a curated network of properties and can help shortlist locations based on the furniture, creative direction and type of imagery required. Our team also handles the on-the-ground coordination, logistics and permits where required.

When shooting in an existing property, we check with the venue in advance about what can be moved, what needs to remain in place and how the furniture can be brought into the space. This keeps the shoot running smoothly and makes sure the location is properly prepared before production begins.
